About

Artist & muralist in Coeur d'Alene

I’m Reese, a mural and graffiti artist based in Coeur d’Alene. I’ve painted grocery stores, guest houses, sheds, and even a shuttle bus. My style is bright and playful, with bold outlines, fun characters, and plenty of flowers. Whether it’s a storefront or a backyard wall, I’ll work with you to make something that fits the space and makes you happy every time you see it.
